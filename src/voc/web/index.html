Complaint labels: LLM against TF-IDF

CFPB complaints, July 2026. An LLM labels each complaint by product and quotes its evidence, and a script checks the quote. It is scored against TF-IDF , a simple word-based model.

LLM, a large language model (here claude-haiku-5-5). It reads the complaint, picks one of the 11 products and copies the sentence that shows why. A script checks that the sentence is really in the complaint.

TF-IDF, a simple word-based model. It learned which words go with each product from 7,024 other complaints, and suggests a product for every complaint. It is the baseline the LLM must beat.

    What is being labelled

    Each complaint is a consumer's own description of a problem. The label is the product the complaint is about: one of the CFPB's 11 products.

    • Checking or savings account
    • Credit card
    • Credit reporting or other personal consumer reports
    • Debt collection
    • Debt or credit management
    • Money transfer, virtual currency, or money service
    • Mortgage
    • Payday loan, title loan, personal loan, or advance loan
    • Prepaid card
    • Student loan
    • Vehicle loan or lease
    The AI's label
    An LLM picks a product and copies the sentence that shows why. A script checks the sentence is really in the complaint. If not, the complaint waits for a person. (LLM, claude-haiku-5-5)
    The word-based model's suggestion
    A simple model that learned which words go with each product from 7,024 other complaints. It is the comparison the AI must beat. (TF-IDF)
    The consumer's own choice
    The product the consumer picked when filing. It is the answer key for scoring, and it is never shown to the AI. (recorded product)

    Glossary

    LLM
    A large language model, here claude-haiku-5-5. It labels each complaint and quotes its evidence.
    TF-IDF
    A simple word-based model, trained on 7,024 other complaints. The baseline the LLM must beat.
    F1
    For one product: a balance of how often its label is right and how many of its complaints are found. From 0 to 1.
    Re-weighted strict accuracy
    Right labels over all complaints, with a quarantined complaint counted as wrong, averaged by each product's share.
    Coverage
    The share of complaints that got a label.
    Quarantine
    Where a label waits for a person when the script could not confirm its quote.
